FAQ

Straight answers.

What does Digital Fuel do?

Digital Fuel is a digital content and performance marketing agency based in Marlborough, Massachusetts. We plan, build and run online advertising for companies across the United States: websites, SEO, Google Ads, Meta ads, content, email and automation.

Which industries does Digital Fuel work with?

We work with companies that sell products and services, and our deepest experience is in home renovation and construction. Our clients include general contractors, painters, electricians, stone fabricators and retailers.

Where is Digital Fuel located, and do you work outside Massachusetts?

Our office is at 2 Mt Royal Ave, Suite 210, Marlborough, MA 01752. We work with clients in every part of the United States.

How do you measure results?

We measure results in leads, calls and sales. Every program includes support and reports, and the Scale and Accelerate programs add call and form tracking with a CRM, so you can see where each lead came from.

How much does it cost to work with Digital Fuel?

The price depends on the program you choose (Grow, Scale or Accelerate) and on your market. You get the exact number in your free strategy session, before you commit to anything.

How long does it take to see results?

Google Ads and Local Services Ads can start bringing calls within days of launch. SEO takes longer, usually a few months, and keeps working after that.

Do you work in Portuguese?

Yes. Our team works in English and Portuguese, and this site is available in both languages.

What happens in the free strategy session?

We learn about your business, your market and your goals, then show you a plan built for your company. The session is free and you decide what happens next.

Do I need a new website to start?

Every program includes a custom website built to generate leads, with hosting and ongoing support.
